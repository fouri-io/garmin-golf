# Overall

## Approach to the round
Felt loose. Hummed Mr. Jones in head to calm nerves and bring me to a Back Nine Mental Space.  Tried to appreciate the walk in between, did not obsess over score.  Only focused on clean backswing and visualizing the ball flight in advance along with throwing the club down the line.  After Analyst, added follow-through to that list as I was pushing right on a few and that helped overall.  Used club data 'actual yardage' from the 'The Turn'. Generally over clubbed as needed but had several shots in the ~160ish range where I have a 7i ~140 and 9W ~180-200 -- used the 6i as well and performed well.  I started lining up on the tee box on the side with my miss so I would angle away from it.  

## Foundations
- Putting was really good -- felt very confident, Number of putts is misleading as I sunk many two putts which would have been three in other rounds from 6-10'. I adopted a slightly more aggressive putt on shorter putts to hit with confidence and if I leave 3' past so be it, at least not short, working really well. Was complimented by both Ed and Steve on putting. 
- Driver - Very good -- Zero penalties due to driver. Only hit dead straight once, but had a predictable fade. I probably need to aim with that more. Often I hit a solid drive but fade would land me in tree cover causing rescue, should have accepted earlier and started aim down left side of fairway.
- Pitching - ok -- good earlier in round, but found a real miss pattern for me which is uphill lie when on the hill going up to green. Consistently hitting way short. Also learned that on dead bermuda, it is hard (like hitting off granite) and the lack of give screwed up several shots -- need to learn a strategy for that.
- Long Clubs - Good -- 9W was a star today , really hitting well. 4 hybrid not bad, no complaints but not the best they have ever been
- Irons - Really interesting today -- so I have calibrated my distance down to use 'The Turn' club data, but must have been hitting very clean as sailed over green several times - four times where I went way past.  Not sure if I am finally getting used to new irons or simply had a very flush day with them.  Only real mishits were on Hole 3.  I found taking the more horizontal swing plane approach really allows clean shots and will work that more into range round -- took the thinking out and was hitting very well (unfortunately paid price with too much distance but not the worst problem to have moving forward I can recalibrate as needed)
