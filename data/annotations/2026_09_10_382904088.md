# Overall

## Approach to the round
Felt loose. Hummed Mr. Jones in head to calm nerves and bring me to a Back Nine Mental Space.  Tried to appreciate the walk in between, did not obsess over score. Implemented Scientist, Artist, 
Analyst Personas.  Only focused on clean backswing and visualizing the ball flight in advance along with throwing the club down the line.  After Analyst, added follow-through to that list as I was pushing right on a few and that helped overall.  Used club data 'actual yardage' from the 'The Turn' instead of my guesstimates and it worked very well -- much closer distances. Generally over clubbed as needed but had several shots in the ~160ish range where I have a 7i ~140 and 9W ~180-200 -- used the 6i on several occasions and performed very well.  Focused on a bit more horizontal club delivery, had a slight fade but generally clean.  I started lining up on the tee box on the side with my miss so I would angle away from it, which really comforted me and worked very well.

## Foundations
- Putting was excellent -- felt very confident, 3-putted once with heavy distance.  Lag from even way off was inches from hole several times.  9' and in were often sunk, believe every 6' I sunk. Realized on some 15' lags I would simply visualize where I would miss for my second putt and instead thought of how to hit in hole and got very close (on 9 lipped from that distance).
- Driver - Very good only used on Hole 1 but hummed Mr. Jones and only thought of backswing and sending to left-center -- striped it straight (no fade woohoo) 228y -- ended up pitching and 1 putt for birdie on H1
- Pitching - very good -- focused on visualizing shot and not only yardage but where I wanted to land it -- noticeably closer to hole -- often within ~20' or so.  Only had one anomaly which I'm not quite sure on H8 I had an uphill lie with a 30y shot (something I'm usually pretty good at), ended up sending it maybe 10y -- I dropped another ball to practice and landed next to it, then a third I put more oomph and put at 24y -- but was strange -- was my 30y to last 45y shot so something with the uphill lie affecting it -- other than that no issues.  Even did a bump and run twice with P and went well.
- Long Clubs - ok -- My 9W I used on H6 and went a bit right and hit tree leaving me short.  I took another one for practice off tee and sent it really well, but pulled a bit.  Used it on H7 and was fantastic had a nice fade and a bit pushback due to wind but started left of tree (intention) and faded nicely around the tree setting me up for a great approach.
- Irons - Good (not great, but not poor and better than usual) -- The new thin grips are really helping my grip.  I now have a bit of a routine where I grip with my left hand, add my right, but make sure in my fingers of both by cocking club a bit with wrists to ensure it is not sitting in palms -- far more comfortable than it was with mid-size grips.  Overall hitting very well with using the yardage adjustments from 'The Turn' instead of my range numbers. I have a true gap at 165-180 currently, but do my best with 6i for now.  I feel more time with my irons will give me a better sense of it over time and maybe incorporate a forgiving 5i or perhaps I start hitting irons cleaner and gain distance -- not doing anything different here, just playing the game I have at the moment.

Really tricky next shot as too far to reliably carry the gulch and about 120 to the gulch -- Went into (one bad shot, save the bogey mode). I took out my PW and hit the ball towards middle of fairway.  Was hoping for near 100y, but only got 74y -- this left a good amount of room to the gulch, but also a long approach shot to the green with ~155y to go.  That is the tricky part on this shot.  1 bad shot, 1 meh shot, put me in : Do whatever you have to do to save double bogey mode.  I took out 6i and hit ok 124y to right of green (not great, but also not my best club), well in play. Left about 12' to hole and ended up sinking the putt for bogey (instead of double) -- so ended very well.
